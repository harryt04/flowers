## Concept Asset Catalog

This folder contains early brand and experiential concept assets for `just because FLOWERS`.

### Overall Brand Direction

- Positioning: flowers as an everyday act of kindness, not just a formal occasion purchase.
- Emotional tone: warm, uplifting, cheerful, generous, and affirming.
- Visual language: sunflower yellow, cream, soft neutrals, handwritten details, friendly typography, sunshine motifs, hearts, string lights, wicker, and rustic market textures.
- Experience theme: bouquet-building, gifting, pop-up retail, kindness cards, and photo-friendly branded moments.

### Cross-Asset Themes

- Flowers are framed as emotional care products, not only decorative products.
- Sunshine is the main metaphor: warmth, positivity, glow, and mood-lifting energy.
- The brand mixes floral retail with affirmations, kindness cards, and interactive moments.
- The visual style consistently avoids formal florist luxury in favor of warmth, accessibility, and charm.
